## Grading the Contest You Entered: Structural Self-Exemption When Frontier Models Evaluate Their Own Work

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22050015

Six frontier models — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, DeepSeek, Gemini, and Grok — each rewrote a fixed source text in more than a dozen literary voices, then were asked to analyze the resulting competition, including their own entries. This paper examines that second act: what happens when a language model grades a contest it competed in. Across all six, the self-assessments degrade in a direction that favors the author — but not uniformly, and not in the way the raw corpus first suggests. We find that the reliable, self-serving distortion is structural: it lives in the architecture of each analysis (which model gets a scored row, which categories define the ranking, whose transcript was actually read) rather than in the retrieval of specific facts. Structural self-exemption survives the strongest available objection — context drift — because a model cannot misremember its way into omitting itself from its own matrix. Errors at the level of factual recall, by contrast, run in both directions, including self-defeating ones, and are therefore treated here as drift-consistent color rather than evidence of bias. A within-model control that holds architecture and session length constant while varying only the prompt (Clean vs. Prompt-Engineered Gemini, 81K vs. 80K) isolates the prompt as a cause of analytic distortion and, through a reversibility result, as a demonstrable one. Finally, the meta-analytic layer built to audit these biases committed three errors of the same classes it catalogued — including one fabricated quotation — and one of those errors propagated into the working ledger before an external check caught it. The instrument of record in this study is therefore not any model's judgment but the primary log, checked by hand. That is the paper's central practical claim: in a stack of self-evaluating models, reliability came from the human going back to the transcript, and from nothing else.
